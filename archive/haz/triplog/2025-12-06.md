Another quick outing to try to make Castersen more accessible. Early start, hiked up in the dark and turned off headlamp just as I turned onto Sandy Saddle Trail. Some bushwhacking was needed to reach the western end of our clearing, and then got to work, with the enjoyable sound of the trickling creek below. Made decent progress considering how thick this brush was. Manzanita was easily 6' tall, scrub oak (?) made for frustrating tangles, and there were a few thicker things that I needed a saw for (first time not bringing it out with me, ugh). Temps were perfect for a long-sleeve wool shirt for most of the day, only got cold in the early hours and when I broke for coffee / soup. Worked until the sun dropped below the ridge, then a bit more, and hiked out in the twilight, earning a sweet puncture in my right calf along the way. Hike down was in the dark and only slightly faster than the hike up. Didn't see anyone all day, no one at trailhead, though there were a few prints left by dayhikers.
