Two weekends in a row, why not :)

Early morning, left house at 3:30am and reached the trailhead minutes before sunrise. Today I wanted to bring all my day's water, my heavy-duty loppers, and a McLeod, so I opted to hike up in shorts & sleeveless and save the long pants and sunshirt for trail maintenance. Barnhardt is still in decent shape and the few plants that brushed my legs didn't cut deep. Reached Sandy Saddle junction with minor sweat drench-age and started clearing a few yards from Barnhardt (I still want to leave a "buffer" of uncleared tread to reduce random wanderers).

Today seemed to drag. Chatting with @ScaredyCat and friends was a nice distraction, and I was enjoying my audiobook, the sun was just relentless and I took a ton of little breaks. Part of me was hoping to clear all the way to my previously-cleared tread, but the dozens of roots that needed to be pulled had other thoughts. So I got as far as I could and decided to call it around 6pm and head down.

Maybe one more weekend and I'll have it cleared to Castersen Seep. Maybe.
