Man, I really wished I would have seen @00blackout 's triplog before leaving the house lol.

Left the house with a 3-day pack with two goals: clean up the 3 short sections of trail that remain to be cleared between Barnhardt Trail and Castersen Seep and haul my tools out. I left them up there in May, with the plan to spend Memorial Weekend up there, but... one family emergency after another and oops it's almost July. One white car at the trailhead, bumped into him maybe a mile up the trail, then it was dry, warm, and pretty quiet for the rest of the hike up. Grabbed my tools and headed to Castersen to set up camp, minutes after the sunset, to find a bone-dry seep. I've never seen it so dry.

I probably could have set up a decent base at Chilson... 7L of water capacity available... but the thought of either breaking camp every day or leaving it for AZT hikers to wonder about didn't sound like fun. So I bundled my tools on my already-laden pack and headed back down. I'll head back up after a few monsoons.

On the upside, the area didn't "feel" dry. The hillsides are still healthily green, flowers a'flowering, etc. And I even heard some water trickling down Barnhardt Canyon on the hike up. I'd bet that some of the more established sources (Club Cabin, Bear Spring, Chilson) also have water. The water table must just be a little too low for Castersen, I guess.
