Owed wife after that whole Thunder River thing so took all three kids up for a day in the Mazzies. Early start, left house at 4:30, got to trailhead around 6am, set up base at 9am. I was the slow one with 12L of water and tools in my pack, plus the falls were flowing and we bumped into Allen, nearby AZT steward, who was doing some (fantastic) maintenance along Barnhardt.

Goal was to wrap up the section of Sandy Saddle that comes up the creek from Castersen, the thickest of the remaining work. Not only are the kids getting very familiar with the area (I sent 9yo & 11yo to the seep to pull 3L of extra water for the day, and they made it down & up without issue on their own!), they were a great help during the day, building cairns, pulling manzanita out as soon as I cut it, and hacking away at some of the nastier roots. Could not have done this section in a day without them. We got it cleared, although we had to leave some roots behind due to time constraints. Walked out around 5:30pm after a long day and did the last two miles by headlamp.

Three small sections to do to finish the Barnhardt -> Castersen segment. It's almost there :DANCE:
