  3. Verde River Trail from Dutchman Grave to Wet Bottom

    Dutchman Grave / Verde River Trail Junction → Wet Bottom / Verde River Trail Junction 7.28 mi +1,235 ft / −1,021 ft

    Route findingNov 5, 2017

    Tons of prints and wide trail to Sycamore Creek, and then nothing. As expected, this (and almost every other) wash meant hunting for a trail on the other bank, because the Mazzies don't believe in straightforward crossings. Trail got faint in Dry Wash, ended up just aiming for the bluffs and picking it back up there. Then things just kinda rolled on until Wet Bottom Creek.

    WaterNov 5, 2017

    Wet Bottom Creek was pretty yet quite dry.

  4. Verde River Trail from Wet Bottom to Red Creek

    Wet Bottom / Verde River Trail Junction → Red Creek / Verde River Trail Junction 0.99 mi +154 ft / −307 ft

    Route findingNov 11, 2018

    Awkward shuffle to find the start of the trail along the bank, steep climb up loose rocks, and then the sign for the start of Wet Bottom. It was a quick half mile.

    Route findingNov 5, 2017

    From the Wet Bottom Trail junction it was time to head down to the river. Trail gives up there so I rock-hopped to Red Creek Rapids.

  5. Verde River Trail from Red Creek to Highwater

    Red Creek / Verde River Trail Junction → Highwater / Verde River Trail Junction 7.53 mi +1,186 ft / −847 ft

    Route findingNov 11, 2018

    Tread was almost invisible, cairns were tiny and easy to miss, and everything was wildly overgrown. Compared to Wet Bottom and Highwater this was by far the hardest to track. Following it south offered good views of the river below and was mentally exhausting. As soon as the route dropped below the upper banks of the river I was left trail-less so I ambled in the general direction of the rapids. Thick reeds masked the way forward.

  6. Verde River Trail from Deadman Mesa to Highwater

    Highwater / Verde River Trail Junction → Deadman Mesa / Verde River Trail Junction 7.47 mi +1,579 ft / −978 ft

    TreadDec 6, 2018

    It was impeccable. Seriously. It is in at least, if not better, as good of condition as Highwater. Shortly after the junction it cuts right down to the river with a series of rocky switchbacks and then, complete with good cairns, marches along the sandy banks with a few jumps to avoid different obstacles. The first two miles has a lot of bovine traffic, and they stuck to the tread. There were also long stretches of straight path through creosote.

    TreadDec 6, 2018

    Initial climb from the East Verde to Deadman Mesa Trail was steep and rocky and simple to follow.

  7. Verde River Trail from Twin Buttes Trailhead to Deadman Mesa

    Deadman Mesa / Verde River Trail Junction → Twin Buttes Trailhead 10.11 mi +3,084 ft / −403 ft

    TreadDec 6, 2018

    Everyone complains about the rocks on this trail, yet the mud is so much worse. Even a 20' section of mud would add pounds of clay to each boot, weighing me down and throwing my balance off. Thankfully the trail is easy to follow.

    TreadJun 24, 2017

    As others have stated, this trail is rocky. Rocks that rock, rocks that roll. There are some genuinely good sections, where the rolly rocks ease up and you can gaze around without watching your step.

    Route findingJun 24, 2017

    Only had to check GPS once or twice on some faint sections, otherwise this was a breeze to travel.
