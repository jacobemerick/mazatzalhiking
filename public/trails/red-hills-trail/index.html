Every leg below is a GPS track that was walked and recorded on 2 trips between Dec 29, 2017 and Sep 3, 2018. Distance is measured along the recorded line; elevation comes from USGS 3DEP lidar sampled along it. Condition notes carry the date they were observed. A leg with no note is a leg with nothing recorded — not a clear trail.

  1. Red Hills Trail from Midnight to Dutchman Grave

    Red Hills / Dutchman Grave Trail Junction → Midnight / Red Hills Trail Junction 12.56 mi +4,732 ft / −2,239 ft

    Route findingSep 4, 2018

    Initial drop from the ridge was sudden and rocky, then more mines and tailings show up to make for an easy descent on the north bank of the drainage. Quick rock-hop and a tread picks up on the south bank and makes for a very easy walk. I had some trouble following the tread after it swings south, likely due to the recent rains smoothing out the dirt, so I spent more time cairn-hunting than I wanted to. A few overgrown sections and then this trail was done.

    Route findingSep 3, 2018

    Drop to Wet Bottom Creek is getting downright easy. Was able to track tread and cairns the entire way down. Cairns and tread were well-defined for half the climb out, then deadfall and manzanita hide any trace of a trail above the 'throne'.

    WaterSep 3, 2018

    Reached Wet Bottom Creek and it was magnificent, tons of water.

    Route findingSep 3, 2018

    Trail was shockingly well-defined alongside the summit of 5601'. Things got a little sketchy in the drainage on the far side and I focused on speed instead of hunting cairns, hopping in the drainage. Did see a few stretches of tread on the banks, found them to be mostly overgrown and not worth the effort. Trail got much easier to track once it climbed up the saddle and left the drainage for good. Cairns got harder to find in the waning light, especially since some of the rock piles were marking old mines instead of route.

    Route findingDec 29, 2017

    500 yards west of the Midnight junction (at the saddle) is where things started to get dicey, and it did so slowly. First it got overgrown with some deadfall, though the tread and cairns were still easy to pick out. Then it got more dicey. A thousand yards to Wet Bottom Creek, while dancing along the ridge, is when elk tracks and two sets of cairns all diverged.

    WaterDec 29, 2017

    Found the anticipated majestic pool of awesome at Wet Bottom Creek all but dried up.

  2. Red Hills Trail from Brush to Midnight

    Midnight / Red Hills Trail Junction → Brush / Red Hills Trail Junction 2.66 mi +969 ft / −277 ft

    TreadDec 29, 2017

    Trail conditions continued to be great even after leaving the AZT on the far side, where footprints faded and horse tracks took over. Another stand of mostly-untouched pines lurk right before the drop off. That drop is ridiculously steep. Path was easy to follow all the way to the old corral, and the trail on to Midnight junction is easy.

    WaterDec 29, 2017

    There was barely anything trickling through Fuller Seep.
