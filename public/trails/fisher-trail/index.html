  1. Fisher Trail from Brody Seep to Mazatzal Divide

    Fisher / Brody Seep Trail Junction → Fisher / Mazatzal Divide Trail Junction 3.62 mi +2,011 ft / −1,227 ft

    Route findingMay 6, 2017

    Junction w/ Brody was difficult to pin down but the tread picks up in a few dozen yards and is easy to follow down to the wash. I tried briefly to pick up the path on the banks and then just followed the wash. Eventually hacked across and found a single lonely cairn in the middle of the next wash, first cairn sighting on Fisher. Did not find the exact junction with Mazatzal Divide, though I'd guess my path was close to it.

    BrushMay 6, 2017

    This wash makes a sudden, scenic drop and forces the trail to head up a manzanita-and-deadfall choked slope towards crumbling cliffs. Tread can be picked up a few times, but it's easiest just to hack up where possible. Below the cliffs there are some rock slides that seemed to knock out the trail. Picked it up again on the other side and more-or-less followed it along the steep slope until the next wash. Again stuck to the wash, as the banks had manzanita well over my head. Then it was a 500' climb through manzanita and deadfall, with a faint tread weaving back and forth in tight switchbacks to a saddle.

    DeadfallMay 6, 2017

    Quick drop and then a 1000' climb up a steep tributary of South Fork Deadman. Didn't even try to find the trail at this point, not with all the pine deadfall, just stuck to the creek and balanced from one log to the next.

    WaterMay 6, 2017

    Found water and a steep waterfall near the fork, no water at Fisher Spring.
