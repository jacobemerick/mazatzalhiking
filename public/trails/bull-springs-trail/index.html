Every leg below is a GPS track that was walked and recorded on 2 trips between Dec 29, 2017 and Dec 5, 2018. Distance is measured along the recorded line; elevation comes from USGS 3DEP lidar sampled along it. Condition notes carry the date they were observed. A leg with no note is a leg with nothing recorded — not a clear trail.

  1. Bull Springs Trail from LF Ranch to Brush

    LF Ranch → Brush / Bull Springs Trail Junction 3.26 mi +1,926 ft / −213 ft

    TreadDec 5, 2018

    The haul up and over Copper Mountain is better defined than I remembered. The tread on this trail is smooth and easy enough to let the eyes wander, a rare treat in the Mazzies.

    TreadDec 29, 2017

    Had braced myself for a tough downhill on this trail and was pleasantly surprised to find it sandy, gradual, and easy on the knees. Last mile got a bit rocky and hard to track by headlamp, especially the little hop over Copper Mountain.

  2. Bull Springs Trail from Brush to Bull Trap Springs

    Brush / Bull Springs Trail Junction → Bull Trap Springs 4.15 mi +661 ft / −1,239 ft

    Route findingDec 5, 2018

    Trail began to fade immediately after the AZT junction, which is to be expected, though it was never hard to track through the waist-high brush, even without a cairn in sight. Beyond the pass there are two minor drainages to swing through, the first of which has an old mining exploration and trailside tank to check out, before the drop into the valley of Bull Spring(s). Struggled a bit to find a steady tread in the area.

    WaterDec 5, 2018

    Took a break at Bull Spring to pull up to full capacity.
