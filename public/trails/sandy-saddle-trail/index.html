    BrushJun 30, 2026

    Two goals: clean up the 3 short sections of trail that remain to be cleared between Barnhardt Trail and Castersen Seep and haul my tools out.

    WaterJun 30, 2026

    Headed to Castersen to set up camp, minutes after the sunset, to find a bone-dry seep. I've never seen it so dry. The area didn't 'feel' dry. The hillsides are still healthily green, flowers a'flowering, etc. The water table must just be a little too low for Castersen, I guess.

    BrushMay 23, 2026

    Started clearing a few yards from Barnhardt (I still want to leave a buffer of uncleared tread to reduce random wanderers). Part of me was hoping to clear all the way to my previously-cleared tread, but the dozens of roots that needed to be pulled had other thoughts. Maybe one more weekend and I'll have it cleared to Castersen Seep. Maybe.

    BrushMay 16, 2026

    I was able to finish off that log and clear most of that section of trail (still a cluster of skinny manzanita that needs to get cut back), and then I slowly walked back to camp, pulling up a few extra roots, re-stacking some cairns, and improving the creek crossings.

    DeadfallMay 16, 2026

    Tracked down exactly where the trail crosses Barnhardt Creek for future trips. Had to bushwhack uphill to find the tread, but once found, I was able to crawl along it (lots of deadfall) and find where it met the creek. That's going to be a beautiful section of trail once cleared.

    DeadfallMay 15, 2026

    My main focus for this trip was going to be THE log... there's a large pine that fell 10+ years ago along the trail tread, and I was tired of balancing on it while getting shoved around with manzanita. I cut down manzanita on the downhill side, then chopped/saw/hacked/cried through the trunk until I was able to break the bottom third through and roll it off trail.

    BrushJan 17, 2026

    Progress was slow, there were a lot of small manzanitas, which meant more clipping, more throwing, and more roots to pull up. I was starting to accept that this last section of trail would take more than the planned three days so I began partial clearing just to make a semblance of a route, and will plan on polishing on future outings.

    BrushSep 14, 2025

    BrushJun 21, 2025

    Venturing down the first part of Sandy Saddle brought out a ton of complaints from the kids -- bushwhacking through manzanita is a bit harder when it's all taller than you. Saw some evidence of recent slashes along the way. Made some more progress on the northern tip, where I stopped last time, though we didn't quite make it to the edge of the ridge. With no sizeable deadfall and less brush density we did make respectable progress (cleared ~200').

    DeadfallApr 26, 2025

We then continued to clear, cutting deadfall, trimming manzanita, and often uprooting plants that were in the tread itself. After 4 hours we had a respectable path cleared from the drainage to the camp.

    BrushApr 25, 2025

    Route findingJun 3, 2018

    Tried to be more conscious of following the old tread this time, confident that I had it all the way to Castersen. Few switchbacks at the beginning that I didn't notice on my last hike. Overall it can be tracked with a careful eye, a GPS definitely helps, and it will make you bleed.

    BrushSep 1, 2017
