    Deadman / Davenport Wash Trail Junction → Deadman / Willow Springs Trail Junction 6.10 mi +3,002 ft / −1,797 ft

    Route findingSep 3, 2017

    Getting to the junction is easy to follow, and there is a good path w/ cairns that lead down to Horse Creek. And then it disappears. Tried going up and down the banks a few time to find where it climbs and eventually just hacked up the hill. It's frustrating, because there are two old barbed fences to cross, and one would think that there'd be a gate or cairn or something to mark where you're supposed to pass through them - nothing. Tread shows up at the next drainage crossing and is easy to follow for the next mile, then gets faint on the long drop to Deadman Creek.

    WaterSep 3, 2017

    Deadman Creek seems to be dependable here, with lots of friendly trees and some reeds growing around the trickling waters.

    Route findingSep 3, 2017

    Trail was hard to track on the other side of Deadman Creek - I crossed, got to the corral, and then followed the fence east, and then lost it. Think I should have gone further east. Hacked my own way up some turrible brush and then picked a route up the hill. Found a few cairns but the tread wasn't trackable for too long.
