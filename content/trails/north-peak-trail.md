### 2017-03-26 brush {source="haz/triplog/2017-03-26.md"}
Above the climb has been recently trimmed, sometime in the last year or so, and I didn't have any difficulty until the camp on 6178.

### 2017-03-26 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-03-26.md"}
At the camp on 6178 manzanita and deadfall took over the path, and while someone had tied pretty pink markers along the path and even knocked down a few bushes, I still lost it and wandered aimlessly in the wash for a bit.
Had to use GPS to get back and make it to The Park.
