## South Fork Deer Creek to Davey Gowan Camp {#17}

### 2017-05-20 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-20.md"}
The first four miles of this is a breeze.
Everything is well trimmed and easy to follow, and even the gravesite has also been cleaned up.
Things start to get hairy after 4.5 miles.
The trail begins to criss-cross the creek, with massive cairns to mark the crossings, though the actual paths on the banks are not always easy to pick up.
Ivy and deadfall complicate things.
It's not terrible, just something to be aware of.
