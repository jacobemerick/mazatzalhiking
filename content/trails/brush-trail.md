## Brush Trail {#2R}

### 2017-12-29 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-12-29.md"}
First mile was beautiful and gave me a few good views north, then the second mile got a bit steep and rocky and I had to slow down.
All in all this trail was easy to follow, and with the exception of that one steep rocky section during the first half, downright enjoyable.
