## Sears to Davenport Wash {#0X}

### 2017-11-18 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-11-18.md"}
Sheep Creek Trail from Sears to Davenport is a bit easier to track than the eastern part of Sears Trail, with small cairns and faint tread to track, but it was easy to lose near the washes.
I did find it interesting how small most of the cairns are, as if someone recently did some revival work, there's just not enough traffic out this way to sustain it.
Sheep Creek is 90% tiny cairn tracking and sorting through game trails.

### 2017-11-18 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-11-18.md"}
That last drop down the ridge after the Sears Junction is terrible, all rolling rocks that really know how to tweak knees.

## Copper Camp Flat to Sears {#0W}

### 2017-11-18 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-11-18.md"}
The final climb was quick and easy.
Drop was easier this time around, though the trail was a bit hard to track along the creek with the fall leaves.

### 2017-11-18 water {source="haz/triplog/2017-11-18.md"}
Few pools along the way that dried up before the seep proper.
Reached the cabin area to find a dry Round Spring and creek.

### 2017-04-15 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-04-14.md"}
Quickly climbed out of the burn area with none of the deadfall or route-finding issues that the end of #87 had.
Most of the trail was broad and easy to roll down.
Last few hundred feet of descent began to get a bit choked up.

### 2017-04-15 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-04-14.md"}
Creek section was fantastic, and the trail criss-crossed a few times and was well-defined.
Beyond the end of Cypress Ridge the desert took over from the trees and the banks became thorny and nasty.
Trail seemed harder to follow so I began switching between hopping along the rocks in the wash, connecting game trails, and hunting for cairns.
Then the old fence line led right to Sheep Creek proper.

## Thicket Spring to Copper Camp Flat {#0U}

### 2017-11-18 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-11-18.md"}
Getting to Squaw Flat Spring was easy, though I still haven't found a track I like from there to the Copper Camp junction.

### 2017-06-11 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-06-11.md"}
Headed to Squaw Flat Spring to complete this segment of the trail and was surprised at how easy the way was.
Few deadfall, some overgrowth, clear tread.

### 2017-06-11 water {source="haz/triplog/2017-06-11.md"}

### 2017-04-14 water {source="haz/triplog/2017-04-14.md"}
I pushed on to Squaw Flat Spring expecting to find more water there and didn't.
