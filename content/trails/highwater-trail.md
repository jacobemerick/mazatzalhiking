As the Verde River Trail heads north it crosses the river twice, once at Red Creek Rapids and then again near Pete's Cabin Mesa.
These crossings are difficult during any time of the year and may become threatening during high water events, so this trail, combined with a section of Wet Bottom, can be used to avoid the crossings.
Highwater Trail can also be combined with other trails to create enjoyable loops in the Mazatzal lowlands.

## Highwater Trail {#2O}

### 2018-12-06 water {source="haz/triplog/2018-12-05.md"}
Watered up at Canyon Creek.

### 2018-11-11 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2018-11-11.md"}
Tread was initially difficult to find, and the cairns, while big, had a tendency to shyly duck behind prickly pear and brush.
Once it dropped off the mesa things got better.
In fact, the trail was overall well-defined and in good shape (that drop into Canyon Creek was a work of art), although every wash was your typical Mazzie route-finding challenge.

### 2018-11-11 brush {source="haz/triplog/2018-11-11.md"}
Pete's Cabin Mesa was some of the most verdant green I've seen in the lowlands and, as the trail entered this area, I was walled in by vegetation that felt more like an immature Midwest forest than usual open desert.
