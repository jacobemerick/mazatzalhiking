## Mazatzal Divide (N) to Chilson Camp {#08}

## Chilson Camp to Fisher {#0G}

### 2017-05-06 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-06.md"}
Started easy enough, a huge cairn marking a rocky slide down to and over the wash below.
Trail was reasonably easy to pick out, tread holding up well and a few cairns scattered about.

### 2017-05-06 brush {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-06.md"}
Some overgrowth will force minor detours.

### 2017-05-06 water {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-06.md"}
Brody Seep was difficult to reach but well worth it.

### 2017-05-06 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-06.md"}
Above the seep it looks like there were two slides that knocked out a section of trail years and years ago and I ended up bushwhacking for a few hundred terrible yards.

## Fisher to Mazatzal Divide (S) {#0H}

### 2017-05-06 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-05-06.md"}
Final leg up to Mazatzal Divide Trail was short and painless, even found some old log steps (somewhat charred) along the route.
