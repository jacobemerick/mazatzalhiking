A steep climb up a manzanita hillside from Cornucopia to Thicket Spring Trail.
A segment of Arizona Trail Passage 22 (recently re-routed) and an integral part of the classic Cornucopia - Thicket Spring Loop.
The 'trailhead' is not easily accessible unless you have a vehicle that can handle 25A.
