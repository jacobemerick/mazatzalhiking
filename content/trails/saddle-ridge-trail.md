## Twin Buttes Trailhead to Whiterock Mesa {#2V}

### 2018-12-05 tread {source="haz/triplog/2018-12-05.md"}
Once my eyes adjusted from the two-track to a single track it was pretty easy going.
Cairns are big and tread is (usually) easy to make out, a dark line in the light grass.
Smooth sailing, easy going over the pleasant trail.
