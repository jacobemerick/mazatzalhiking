Short and barely-used trail above the Verde River that leads to an old cabin along Sheep Creek.
It is difficult to access and offers few loop options, although the final destination is well worth the venture.

## Fig Trail {#1K}

### 2018-02-11 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2018-02-11.md"}
Found the twisted stump marking the beginning and followed a shockingly well-defined tread east.
We'd follow the tread few a few hundred yards, get slightly confused and/or make stupid decisions, and then pick it back up a short time later.
Along the way I built a few cairns.

### 2018-02-11 tread {source="haz/triplog/2018-02-11.md"}
The old road that the trail follows is washed out at the second wash crossing and we had to find our own terrible way up the other bank, and then we re-discovered the trail on the other side of the next hill.
