Beautiful trail in the lower Mazatzals, spending much of its time dancing along a little creek before swinging around the foothills and connecting near Mormon Grove Trailhead.
Part of the Arizona Trail and receives regular maintenance.

## Cross F Trailhead to Sunflower {#1F}

### 2021-02-27 water {source="haz/triplog/2021-02-27.md"}
Happily trickling creek next to the trail.

### 2017-06-11 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2017-06-11.md"}
Easy to follow as it snakes through a pleasant little forest.
