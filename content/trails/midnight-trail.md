Connector trail deep in the Mazatzal Wilderness with several appealing features of its own. A fundamental piece of the Midnight Mesa Loop and could be used for longer, multi-day trips that include Red Hills and Deadman Trail.

## Midnight Trail {#2F}

### 2018-09-03 deadfall {source="haz/triplog/2018-09-03.md"}
The few cairns did little to ease the pain of deadfall, rolling rocks, and thick brush.
Once the trail enters unburnt forest things do get easier.

### 2018-09-03 water {source="haz/triplog/2018-09-03.md"}
Each drainage, including Wet Bottom Creek, had fresh water running.

### 2018-09-03 tread {source="haz/triplog/2018-09-03.md"}
Trail changed dramatically on the north side, with wide tread and large cairns.
