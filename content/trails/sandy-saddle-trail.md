This trail provides an option to the upper two miles of Barnhardt Trail and makes a connection along the Mazatzal Divide that is perfect for hikers aiming for Horse Camp Seep or Deadman Falls.
It can be combined with Barnhardt to do a challenging 15 mile dayhike or any number of longer loops involving Rock Creek Trail or even some of the western trails.

My main focus for this trip was going to be THE log... there's a large pine that fell 10+ years ago along the trail tread, and I was tired of balancing on it while getting shoved around with manzanita.
I cut down manzanita on the downhill side, then chopped/saw/hacked/cried through the trunk until I was able to break the bottom third through and roll it off trail.
