## LF Ranch to Brush {#2S}

### 2018-12-05 tread {source="haz/triplog/2018-12-05.md"}
The haul up and over Copper Mountain is better defined than I remembered.
The tread on this trail is smooth and easy enough to let the eyes wander, a rare treat in the Mazzies.

### 2017-12-29 tread {source="haz/triplog/2017-12-29.md"}
Had braced myself for a tough downhill on this trail and was pleasantly surprised to find it sandy, gradual, and easy on the knees.
Last mile got a bit rocky and hard to track by headlamp, especially the little hop over Copper Mountain.

## Brush to Bull Trap Springs {#2Q}

### 2018-12-05 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2018-12-05.md"}
Trail began to fade immediately after the AZT junction, which is to be expected, though it was never hard to track through the waist-high brush, even without a cairn in sight.
Beyond the pass there are two minor drainages to swing through, the first of which has an old mining exploration and trailside tank to check out, before the drop into the valley of Bull Spring(s).
Struggled a bit to find a steady tread in the area.

### 2018-12-05 water {source="haz/triplog/2018-12-05.md"}
Took a break at Bull Spring to pull up to full capacity.
