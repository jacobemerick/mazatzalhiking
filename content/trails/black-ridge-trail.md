Old rancher trail that climbs up the side of Black Ridge and drops down the other side, first to Brunson Tank, then continuing on to Upper Sheep Creek Spring.
While this is an unofficial route, it is in good shape and is well-used by both foot and hoof traffic.
Can be used to make a convenient dayhike loop from Cross F Trailhead.

## Black Ridge Trail {#1G}

### 2021-02-27 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2021-02-27.md"}
Trail is well-defined going up that ridge.
Plenty of forks to choose from on top, had to check gps a few times.
