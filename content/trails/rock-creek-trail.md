## Rock Creek Trail {#03}

### 2020-03-21 brush {source="haz/triplog/2020-03-21.md"}
There were a few sections of new overgrowth on the climb up to the creek crossing, nothing worth fretting about.

### 2020-03-21 water {source="haz/triplog/2020-03-21.md"}
Yeah the falls were flowing.

### 2020-03-21 tread {source="haz/triplog/2020-03-21.md"}
Snow became a minor issue after 6000', hiding tread and adding a few slips.

### 2020-03-21 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2020-03-21.md"}
Descent to Hopi is 95% defined with a narrow break in the manzanita and a few cairns.

### 2016-09-23 tread {source="haz/triplog/2016-09-23.md"}
The trail maintenance is on point.
There were sections, especially through the never-ending-hill-climb, that looked better than the AZT.
And once you get up to rocky land the designers really start showing off.

### 2016-09-23 water {source="haz/triplog/2016-09-23.md"}
Was cramping while filtering from the rock pools.

### 2016-09-23 route-finding {source="haz/triplog/2016-09-23.md"}
The last thousand feet, and the descent beyond, was torturous, especially as the trail started to fade in and out.
And yeah, the descent down to Hopi Springs is nonexistent.

### 2016-09-23 brush {source="haz/triplog/2016-09-23.md"}
Manzanitas chewed up my shins something fierce all the way to Mazatzal Divide.
