A roundabout climb that swings below and next to the bulk of Mazatzal Peak, starting from Barnhardt Trail and meeting the Mazatzal Divide a few miles south of the Barnhardt Trail junction.
An integral part of the Mazatzal Peak Loop, and also used sometimes to summit the peak itself.
